# Business-context interview

Ask only what changes interpretation or action, in short themed batches using ordinary chat or the supported question interface. Skip answered questions. Optional document/transcript sources need a user-scoped request.

- Purpose: What outcome matters most this quarter/year? Which metrics and targets are actually tracked?
- Customers: B2B/B2C mix, buyer intent, minimum quantities/order value, repeat research visits, strongest market, personas.
- Misleading metrics: Which page behaviors look like abandonment but are intentional? Any prior analysis that misunderstood the store?
- Priorities: Where does the team suspect loss? Is each concern measured or a hypothesis?
- Stack: Store/checkout platform, customizations, fragile components, payments, apps, staging, repository access.
- Delivery: Who can implement recommendations? How do review, deployment, and rollback work?
- Support: Who triages findings, and where?
- Brand: Voice, spelling conventions, verified shipping/promotion copy, wording to avoid.
- Working preferences: Detail, directness, desired deliverables, and when the team wants to review a plan.
- Closing: What two or three facts should future analysis never get wrong?

Do not fill private goals, money figures, staff counts, deployment behavior, or platform settings from public inference. Mark unknowns for confirmation.
